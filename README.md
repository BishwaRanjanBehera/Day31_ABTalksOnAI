# Day31_ABTalksOnAI - Output
Day31_ABTalksOnAI

A fully self-contained, single-file dashboard game — dark control-tower theme, 3-minute shift, 7 live KPIs, 10 alert types with escalating frequency, action-based scoring with delayed consequences, pause/help/sound controls, and a final grade screen with Play Again. Just open the file in any browser — no dependencies needed.

🚀 Built an AI-powered "Supply Chain Control Tower" simulation — in a single HTML file.

As a Senior TPM working across Payments, Banking, and Enterprise Tech programs, I wanted to explore how far a single AI prompt could go in turning a real operational scenario into an interactive experience. So I asked an AI to design and build a game simulating life as a Head of Operations managing a global supply chain in real time.

The ask: A self-contained web app (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS only, no frameworks or backend) simulating a live ops control center — dark, dashboard-style UI with animated KPI cards, a rotating stream of disruption alerts, and consequence-driven decision-making.

What came back:
📊 7 live KPIs — Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transportation Efficiency, Operating Cost, Revenue Protected, and Score
🚨 10 realistic disruption types — port congestion, supplier delays, truck breakdowns, demand spikes, customs holds, factory failures, and more
⚡ 8 response actions per alert (Expedite, Reroute, Backup Supplier, Air Freight, Ignore, etc.), each with different — and sometimes delayed — consequences
⏱️ A 3-minute live shift with escalating alert frequency and multiple simultaneous incidents, just like a real control tower under pressure
🏆 A performance report at the end — final score, KPI breakdown, and a letter grade (A+ to D)

It's a small project, but a good reminder of how quickly AI can translate an operational mental model — the kind you build over 20 years managing real programs — into something tangible, interactive, and shareable.
